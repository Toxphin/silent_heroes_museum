01 / THE IDEA

Not every mission ends
with a return journey.

When a spacecraft completes its work, it may stay where it landed, continue in orbit, or become a silent marker of a mission that changed what we know.

Space's Silent Heroes documents these machines with care: what they were built to do, what happened to them, and why their presence still matters.

“A mission can end. The discovery it leaves behind can keep moving us forward.”

05 / MISSION LOG

A timeline of departure.

From the first lunar landings to robotic exploration on Mars and beyond, each mission left a trace or a lasting record.

1969

Apollo 11 reaches the Moon

The Lunar Module's descent stage remains at the landing site after the ascent stage departs.

1971–72

Apollo rovers extend exploration

Electric lunar vehicles carry astronauts across the surface during Apollo 15, 16 and 17.

1976

Viking landers operate on Mars

Viking 1 and Viking 2 return landmark images and science from the Martian surface.

1997

Sojourner explores Mars

The Pathfinder mission demonstrates mobile robotic exploration on another planet.

2001

NEAR Shoemaker lands on Eros

The spacecraft completes its mission at the surface of asteroid 433 Eros.

2018

Dawn's mission concludes

After exploring Vesta and Ceres, Dawn remains in orbit around Ceres.

2024

Ingenuity's flight campaign ends

After 72 flights on Mars, rotor damage ends the helicopter's flight operations.
